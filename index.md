## <div align="center"><font color="#ff0000">Rules</font></div>

### <font color="#ffc000">Name</font>
**<font color="#ffc000">Name only in English(USA)</font>**  
**<font color="#ffc000">Exceptions:</font>**  
**<font color="#ffc000">If cannot find English title</font>**  
**<font color="#ffc000">If original languaue Tite is more recognazible than english one</font>**

### <font color="#00ffff">Links</font>
**<font color="#00ffff">Allowed content database sources:</font>**  
**<font color="#ff0000">ČSFD - only "csfd.cz" (csfd.sk forbidden); link ends only with "/prehled" or nothing ".../"</font>**  
**<font color="#ffff00">IMDB - only clean link not with referal part of link</font>**  
**<font color="#ff00ff">Network - link to official network of title (ex. ReelShort)</font>**

### <font color="#ff0000">My Rating Position (MRP)</font>
**<font color="#ff0000">MRP rating is allowed only on titles with 103% or more</font>** **<font color="#00ff00">Average Rating</font>**

### <font color="#A546EC">Seen</font>
**<font color="#A546EC">Seen format:</font>**  
**<font color="#A546EC">[Full Times Seen OR Uncertain Watched Times] + [Watched with TVM] - [Watched episodes / minites]; [Watched episodes / minites]</font>**  

**<font color="#A546EC">Letter prefixes of titles with uncertain watched times:</font>**  
**<font color="#A546EC">Q = 3+ (count as 4 in SeenCalc)</font>**  
**<font color="#A546EC">X = 3</font>**  
**<font color="#A546EC">Y = 2</font>**  
**<font color="#A546EC">Z = 1</font>**  

**<font color="#A546EC">If title is going to be watched 3rd time with CZ dabing or more must be seen in original / English dabing.</font>**

### <font color="#ff00ff">Shorts AI Rating</font>
**<font color="#ff00ff">Shorts that are AI (with "AI" tag) is maximum</font>** **<font color="#00ff00">AR = 50%</font>**
